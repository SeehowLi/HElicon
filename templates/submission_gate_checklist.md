# Submission Gate Checklist

## Blocking findings

- [ ] Immutable set is intact.
- [ ] Threat model and leakage boundary are explicit and consistent.
- [ ] Material claims have adequate evidence and valid scope.
- [ ] Citation existence and attribution are externally verified where required.
- [ ] Required machine checks pass or have an explicit `NOT_RUN` reason.

## Warnings

- [ ] Venue fit and contribution hierarchy are coherent.
- [ ] Evaluation covers claimed workloads, baselines, parameters, and costs.
- [ ] Thin style baseline is labeled and produces no drift warning.
- [ ] Page compression preserved threat-model and claim qualifiers.

## Optional improvements

- [ ] P4–P6 language passes are complete where useful.
- [ ] Remaining surface edits are semantic-free.
- [ ] Rebuttal or camera-ready promises are feasible and tracked.
